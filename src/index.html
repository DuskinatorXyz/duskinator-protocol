Questions

Does it need my wallet?+

No. There is no connect button. You read, then you leave.

Why show a floor figure?+

Prices drift while a transfer waits in line. The floor deducts a half-percent allowance from the estimate.

Why would a pair return nothing?+

A token can be listed yet have no link toward your target. Silence means nothing is routable right now at that size.
